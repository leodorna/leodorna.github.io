About

I'm Leonardo da Luz Dorneles, a Ph.D. candidate at the Institute of Informatics (INF) of the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS), in Porto Alegre, Brazil, and a member of the SBCB Lab (Structural Bioinformatics and Computational Biology). I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering and my Master's degree in Computer Science from UFRGS, and I was a guest researcher in the Lattice Boltzmann Research Group at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), Germany.

My research interests are high-performance computing, matheuristics, multi-objective optimization and fluid dynamics simulation. Much of my recent work applies facility location models to access to acute stroke care, as part of the MAPSTROKE project. The full list is on the research page.
